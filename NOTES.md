- Engine: the `.stats` grid survives a narrow phone. Three lines of CSS, all of them the same bug from different angles: `grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr` → `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`, `min-width: 0` on `.stat`, and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on `.stat b`. A `1fr` track is `minmax(auto, 1fr)` and a grid item defaults to `min-width: auto`, so *both* floored at min-content — the longest single word in a value. One long word therefore widened its own column, squeezed the other, and when the pair no longer fit, pushed the whole page sideways. Flooring both at 0 stops the column being dragged wide; `overflow-wrap: anywhere` lets the word itself break rather than stick out of its tile, and it is `anywhere` rather than `break-word` on purpose — only `anywhere` also shrinks the element's min-content size, which is what the grid measures. One column below 360px: at 320px two columns leave 124px for 18px type, which is three words a line.
